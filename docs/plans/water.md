## The original's order (from `generation-pipeline.ts`)

grid → heightmap → markupGrid → depressionLakes → nearSeaLakes (**these five are already ported**) → **mapSize (`Coordinates.generate`)** → **temperatures** → **precipitation** → clearPack → **regraph (`Pack.generate`)** → **markupPack** → **rivers (`Rivers.generate`)** → biomes → … The bold steps are this plan. Everything after rivers (biomes, ice, cultures, burgs, states, …) is outside it.

## The stages, each tested before the next, each with a step page and fixtures from the original's own code

The order is the original's, and **is not reordered for convenience.**

1. **Coordinates** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-1-coordinates.md))* (`Coordinates.generate`): where the map sits on the globe (its size and shift from the template key, with the random draws `P` and `gauss`), then the latitude box (`latT`, `latN`, `latS`). **The random state it starts from is established by capturing the original's real chain with checkpoints of the generator's state, not assumed to be the height generator's last state.** The production API defines explicitly where the stream is reset and where it continues, **including when the work starts from a loaded `LandMap`** (which stores no random state).
2. **Temperature** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-2-temperature.md))*: an `Int8` per grid cell from its row's latitude and its height.
3. **Precipitation** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-3-precipitation.md))*: a `Uint8` per grid cell: the winds enter by the sides and edges and drop humidity cell by cell, with the draws `rand(10, 20)` from the stream reset to the seed, the winds from six angles (default 225, 45, 225, 315, 135, 315) and the rain level (default 100); the quirk of skipping a band that starts at cell 0 is kept.
4. **The packed graph (`Pack.generate`)** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-4-packed-graph.md))*: deep-ocean points dropped, non-coastal lake points dropped, extra points added along the coast, then a second Voronoi diagram (the package's own, already ported); each packed cell remembers its grid cell `g`, its height and its area. **The number of packed points is budgeted before any allocation.**
5. **`markupPack`** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-5-packed-features.md))*: the packed graph's features, the distance field `t`, `haven` and `harbor`, feature vertex rings, and for a lake its `shoreline` and `height`.
6. **The lake helpers, ported and tested on their own** *(done 2026-10-20; [step page](../steps/water-6-lake-helpers.md))* (`getHeight` and `defineShoreline` were needed by `markupPack` and are already ported with stage 5, as internal helpers of `PackedFeatures`; the others remain): `getHeight`, `defineShoreline`, `defineClimateData`, `detectCloseLakes`, `cleanupLakeData`. **They are not run as a "preparation stage" before the rivers.** In the original they run inside `Rivers.generate`, in this order, and the port keeps that order (stage 7).
7. **`Rivers.generate`, in the original's execution order** *(budgets, refusals and sub-stages: [water-7-rivers.md](water-7-rivers.md); 7a [working heights](../steps/water-7a-working-heights.md), 7b [geometry](../steps/water-7b-river-geometry.md) and 7c [the whole generator](../steps/water-7c-river-generator.md) built 2026-10-21)*: reset the generator to the seed; `alterHeights` (the working copy of the packed heights); `detectCloseLakes`; `resolveDepressions` (up to 250 iterations: **bounded before it runs**); `drainWater` (which calls `defineClimateData` with the **working** heights; flux from the rain, lake outlets, downhill flow, confluences and tributaries); `defineRivers` (river cells, source, mouth, discharge, **and the records' numbers: `addMeandering` with the pathUtils `meander` and its relaxation, `getApproximateLength`, `getSourceWidth`, `getOffset`, `getWidth`**, which are dependencies of the generated records and not later drawing); `calculateConfluenceFlux`; `cleanupLakeData` (after the river records exist: it removes the temporary lake fields and keeps only references to surviving rivers); then applying the working heights and the **downcut** (at most 5, never on lowlands under 35) to the packed heights. **The meander output is budgeted before allocation.** If a stage boundary inside this has to be an intermediate checkpoint, it is described as such and not as a finished `Rivers.generate` port.
8. **Saving and loading** *(done 2026-10-21; [plan](water-8-saving.md), [step page](../steps/water-8-saving-and-loading.md))*: one file holding the land map and the water state; the persisted-state inventory is in the plan.
9. **Drawing** *(rivers built 2026-10-21; [plan](drawing-water.md), [step page](../steps/drawing-water-1-rivers.md); the lakes stay as the land picture shows them)*: the river outline is the original's own shape (filled, Catmull-Rom), trimmed to the drawn land and extended to the drawn coast.

**Outside the generation scope unless a dependency requires it:** the SVG path construction (`getRiverPath`, curve generators), naming and typing (`specify`, `getName`, `getType`, which draw random words), and editing or creating rivers by hand. **This plan stops at rivers, saving and their drawing.** Biomes are a sensible next proposal, not a prerequisite and not an automatic extension of scope; rivers and lakes are useful outputs by themselves.

## What the port adds to the package

- **A separate `PackedMap`,** sharing reusable geometry types and operations with the land layer and **not a mode flag on `LandMap`:** packed cell numbers, the mapping to grid cells, the features and the heights mean different things. **Grid numbers and packed numbers are distinguished at every API boundary** (the mapping `g` is explicit). **The existing grid-based realms stay grid-based; this port does not migrate them.**
- **Heights, stated plainly:** the **grid heights stay unchanged.** The normal upstream generation path **does change the packed heights** (the working copy raised for routing, then the downcut applied to the packed heights). A simple first rendering of rivers and lakes can precede the elaborate river drawing.
- **Settings the original keeps in `options`,** as explicit values with the original's defaults (temperature equator 27 °C, north pole −30, south pole −15; rain 100; the six wind angles; the height exponent 2; `resolveDepressionsSteps` 250; `lakeElevationLimit` 20, already used), and the geography options (map size, latitude and longitude shifts, given or drawn from the template by `Coordinates`). They are validated and saved with the map's recipe.
- **The seeded stream:** the original sets `Math.random = Alea(seed)` at several points; the fixtures show where, and the production API states every reset and continuation explicitly.
- **The persisted-state inventory is decided before stage 8 is implemented,** separating the **final state** (packed points, packed final heights, features, the distance field, the rivers and the final lake fields) from the **temporary lake fields** the original deletes in `cleanupLakeData` (`river`, `enteringFlux`, `outCell`, `closed`, and `inlets` and `outlet` filtered to surviving rivers).

## Parity, tests and evidence

- **Fixtures:** the capture script runs the original's own `Coordinates`, `Temperature`, `Precipitation`, `Pack`, `Features.markupPack`, `Lakes` and `Rivers` on the grid made by the original's own earlier steps (the bundling method of the land layer, with the globals stubbed), for several seeds, templates and cell counts, and stores digests and the full arrays where small. The Swift port is compared **exactly**, stage by stage, so a mismatch can be located.
- **Safety refusals are divergences, and are documented as such:** bounds on the packed points, the depression iterations and the meander output refuse oversized work with named errors; they are not part of the original's behaviour.
- **Invariants are asserted only where the original guarantees them,** accounting for storage wrapping, lake evaporation and the difference between the working heights and the final ones (for example, conservation of water cannot be assumed once lakes evaporate and outlets are conditional).
- **Known numeric traps (from reading):** typed-array storage truncation and wrapping (`Int8`, `Uint8`, `Uint16`, `Uint32` arrays); `rn` rounding; `Math.round` halves; `**` (the temperature uses a power and the lake evaporation uses `exponent`); `Array.sort` stability and the comparators used by the river code (`sort((a, b) => h[b] - h[a])` on a typed list); `mean` and accumulated floating-point sums in the order the original adds them; the quirk noted in the precipitation code (`a band starting at cell 0 is skipped`) must be kept. Each is a place the port can drift and gets its own test.
- Beyond parity: invariants (water conservation where it holds, rivers flow downhill or through lakes, confluence fluxes), determinism, bounded work, validated settings, refusals with named errors, mutation checks, release timings at 10,000 and 100,000 cells, as in the earlier layers.
