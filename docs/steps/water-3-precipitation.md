# Water chain, stage 3: precipitation

Status: built 2026-10-20 (plan: [water.md](../plans/water.md)). Third stage of the faithful port of the original's water chain; it reads the grid's heights and the temperatures stage 2 produces, and the latitude bounds stage 1 produces.

## What was built

- **`Precipitation.compute(for:geography:temperature:settings:)`** (`Sources/FantasyMapCore/Climate/Precipitation.swift`), a port of `precipitation-generator.ts` at the pinned original (commit `8936c9c`): the precipitation of every **grid** cell as the original's `Uint8Array`, so a sum above 255 **wraps** (truncated toward zero, modulo 256) at every store, as it does there. Winds enter the map by its sides: westerly winds from the first cell of each row, easterly winds from its last cell, northerly winds from the first row and southerly winds from the last one. A wind starts with a maximum humidity (120 × the modifier for the side winds, scaled by the row's latitude band; 60 × the modifier × the share of rows sending the wind, scaled by the band of the map's northern or southern edge, or the mean of the table when the map spans more than 60° of latitude, for the vertical ones), gains humidity over water, rains at the coast with a draw `rand(10, 20)`, and over land rains the normal loss plus more for rising ground, until it runs dry or meets ground above 85.
- **The random stream is a fresh `Alea(seed)`**: the original replaces its stream with that at the start ("the same winds for the same map, whoever rolled before"). The only draws are the coastal ones, in the order the winds are passed. The stream after a run is compared with the original's in the tests.
- **`Precipitation.winds(for:geography:settings:)`** returns the rows and columns the winds enter through (the original's `getWinds`): per row its first cell, the band's latitude modifier and the wind tier. The six angles are one for each 30° latitude tier from north to south; the limits are exclusive (westerly over 40 and under 140, easterly over 220 and under 320, northerly over 100 and under 260, southerly over 280 or under 80). A row can send more than one wind.
- **The legacy quirk is kept:** a westerly or easterly band whose first cell is cell 0 is skipped (the original's comment says fixing it changes every map). In practice it is the first row's westerly band; the northerly bands are plain cell numbers and are not skipped.
- **Reads outside the array behave as the original's `undefined`:** a wind that runs on past the end of a row reads the neighbouring row's cell (the original does not stop at the row), and past the first or last cell of the whole array the height is `undefined`, which compares false with everything: not sea, not passable. The port keeps both.
- **`PrecipitationSettings`:** the rain level in percent (default 100) and the six wind angles (default 225, 45, 225, 315, 135, 315). **Package policy, provisional, not upstream limits and not a physical range or a work limit:** the rain level finite 0 … 1000 (higher rain does not necessarily give higher stored precipitation, because the byte wraps, deliberately kept) (the original takes any number not below 0) and each angle finite from 0 up to 360 (the original takes whole degrees 0 … 359; fractions are an explicit package extension, compared with the same exclusive limits; fixture parity covers the captured settings, not every fractional angle).
- **Safety refusals, documented as divergences:** invalid settings and inconsistent inputs (cell counts that disagree, a grid whose cells are not `cellsX × cellsY`, a cell count not above 0, a latitude span outside 0 … 180) are refused with named errors (`invalidSettings`, `invalidInputs`); the original takes the values as given. **The consumed latitude interval is validated as stage 2's is:** `latN` and the southern bound `latN − latT` (rounded to one decimal) must lie within −90 … 90; the stored `latS` is neither trusted nor read. The map size comes from `LandMap.cellsDesired`, as the original's `Grid.getCellsDesired()` reads the option.

## Fixtures and tests

`tools/capture-original-precipitation.mjs` (wired into `tools/capture-original.sh`) runs the original's own `Grid.generate`, `HeightmapGenerator.generate`, `Features.markupGrid`, the two lake steps, `Coordinates.generate`, `Temperature.generate` and then `Precipitation.generate`, and records for **300 runs** the bounds the original had, the temperature's sha256, the winds `getWinds()` found, the cell counts, a sha256 of the Uint8 bytes, the minimum and maximum, and **the next random value after the run** (a matching next-value checkpoint: it shows the stream stands at a matching place, not that the full state or the number of draws is proven identical); 54 runs keep the **whole array**. The runs: the fourteen templates on six map configurations with the default winds and rain; ten wind sets (all west, all east, all from the north, all from the south, the exact limits 40, 140, 220, 320, 100 and 260, the values one inside them, 280 and 80 and their neighbours, and mixed ones) on the whole world and four slices, on three configurations; six rain levels (0, 13.5, 50, 100, 200, 500) on three slices; and whole arrays for different winds and rain on two small maps. The fixtures check: capture twice, the eight earlier fixtures unchanged.

`PrecipitationTests` (18): **all 300 runs equal the original exactly, fed the original's own bounds and our own temperature** (the temperature is first checked equal to the original's, which isolates this stage), with the next-value checkpoint and the winds equal too, **and all 300 again through the whole chain from the map alone**; the 54 whole arrays are equal cell for cell; the captured runs cover both sides of the vertical-wind latitude rule, bands starting at cell 0, dry and wet maps. Hand-computed cases, worked out independently in plain arithmetic: the skipped band; a westerly wind with its coastal draw (the draw is recomputed from a copy of the stream and the stream is checked to have moved by one draw); **the byte wrap** (a cell's coastal rain plus its own rain over 255 stores modulo 256); permafrost at −6 and not at −5; a northerly, a southerly and an easterly wind; rising ground and **a wall above 85** that stops the wind; the vertical latitude modifier from the edge's band and the mean of the table; no rain at level 0; the cell-count scale of the modifier; the six wind tiers, the band indices and the exclusive angle limits; every refusal. (Two of my first hand expectations were wrong: one wind angle belongs to two directions, 45 being westerly and also southerly, and an easterly wind's last cell looks ahead into the previous row's last cell; the tests now say so.)

**Byte wrapping** (a truncated stored value above 255; reaching 255 itself does not wrap) does not occur in the captured runs (the highest value is 194), so the wrap is verified by the hand case and not by the original's output.

## Mutation checks

54 mutations, each made and undone: the legacy skip removed, the band cap, the permafrost limit (both the value and the inequality), the coastal draw (range, offset, the minimum of 1), the water gain and water rain, the sea and passable limits, the evaporation threshold and amount, the wall resetting the humidity, the normal loss divisor and minimum, the rise sign and the 70 divisor, the modifier's powers and the rain level scale, the side winds' maximums, the first cell of an easterly band, the vertical winds' maximums, the 60° rule on either edge, the band and tier arithmetic, the row latitude, every angle limit on each side, the store wrapping versus clamping, the table values, the vertical total and the source cells. **49 were caught at first. Five survived:**

- Two were **gaps** (found and fixed with a test each): a wind that starts exactly dry (humidity 0 must not blow) and the southern edge's switch to the mean over 60° (only the northern edge's was pinned). Both are now caught.
- Three are **equivalent, not gaps**, and the original's code is kept as it is: the lower and the upper clamp of the humidity after a cell's rain (the rain never exceeds the humidity, and the humidity never exceeds the maximum, so neither clamp can bind) and the minimum of 1 in the rain of a land cell (the normal loss is already at least 1).

## Limits

- 300 runs are a sample; exact equality is observed on them, not guaranteed for every input. `pow` is used twice (the quarter power of the cell count and the square of a height ratio); it agreed with V8 on all runs, and for the square on every height (a quarter power by repeated square roots would **not** have matched one tested count, so Foundation's `pow` is the one to keep).
- The precipitation is per **grid** cell, as in the original; the packed graph (stage 4) maps back to it through the grid-cell numbers.
- A rain level of 0 gives all zeros because every wind starts dry; the arithmetic does not produce a not-a-number there (checked by reading the code, and by the captured run).
